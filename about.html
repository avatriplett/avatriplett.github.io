Pomeranians

Pomeranians stand out from other dogs by packing the bold, confident attitude of much larger Spitz-type working and sled ancestors into a tiny, fluffy frame. Despite their delicate toy-dog size, they often act fearless around bigger animals, bark alertly like watchdogs, and show a strong independent streak alongside deep loyalty to their owners. They also sport a thick double coat that comes in roughly two dozen colors and patterns and can shift in appearance as puppies mature into adults.
